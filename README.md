8. Check Search Volume and keyword Difficulty
There are two important points to search focus keywords keyword difficulty and volume.

Search Volume

Search volume shows how often a keyword is searched during a particular period.
For example:
Keyword: SEO tools
Monthly search volume: 10,000

A higher search volume indicates greater potential traffic, but it may also mean stronger competition.

Keyword Difficulty

Keyword difficulty estimates how difficult it may be to rank for a particular keyword.
A keyword with very high difficulty may be challenging for a new website.
Therefore, try to find a balance between:

Search volume + relevance + competition + search intent

For a newer website, targeting specific keywords with reasonable search volume and manageable competition can be a practical approach.

10. Review and Update Your Keywords Regularly
Keyword research should not be a one-time activity. Search trends, user behaviour, competition, and search engine results can change over time. A keyword that performs well today may become less popular later. Use tools such as Google Trends to monitor changes in search interest. You should also review your existing website content periodically.
Check:

Which keywords are generating traffic?
Which pages are ranking?
Which keywords have lost rankings?
Are new related keywords appearing?
Has search intent changed?
Can the article be updated with new information?
Updating content with relevant keywords can help keep your website useful and competitive.

How to Choose the Right Keyword
After collecting a list of keywords, evaluate each keyword using a simple checklist.
Ask yourself:

Is the keyword relevant to my topic?
Does it match my target audience?
What is the search intent?
Does it have reasonable search volume?
Is the competition manageable?
Can my content provide a better answer?
Can the keyword be used naturally in my article?
If the answer is yes to most of these questions, the keyword may be a good choice.

Example of Keyword Research
Suppose you want to write an article about choosing a budget smartphone.
Your initial seed keyword could be:

Smartphone

After research, you might discover more specific keywords:

Budget smartphone
Best budget smartphone
Cheap smartphone
Best smartphone under ₹20,000

Instead of targeting only the broad keyword “smartphone,” you could choose a more specific keyword such as “best smartphone under ₹20,000.”
You can then create content that directly answers the user’s needs, including specifications, important features, comparison points, and buying tips.

Common Keyword Research Mistakes
Avoid these common mistakes when selecting keywords.

Choosing Only High-Volume Keywords

High-volume keywords may have significant competition. Consider relevance and ranking difficulty as well.

Keyword Stuffing

Do not repeatedly insert the same keyword into your content. Search engines prefer natural and helpful content.

Ignoring Search Intent

A keyword may look attractive, but if your content does not satisfy the user’s intent, it may not perform well.

Targeting Too Many Keywords

Trying to target dozens of unrelated keywords in one article can make the content confusing.

Forgetting Long-Tail Keywords

Specific search phrases can help you reach users with clear search intent.

Conclusion
Keyword research is a fundamental part of SEO and an important step in creating successful website content. It helps you understand what your audience searches for and allows you to create content that answers their questions. The most effective approach is to combine several strategies. Start by understanding your audience and identifying seed keywords. Then use keyword search tools, analyse search intent, explore long-tail keywords, study competitors, check Google suggestions, evaluate search volume and difficulty, group related keywords, and regularly review your keyword strategy.

Remember that successful SEO is not simply about choosing the keyword with the highest search volume. The goal is to select relevant keywords that match user intent and allow you to create genuinely useful content. With consistent keyword search and high-quality content, you can build a stronger SEO strategy and improve your website’s chances of attracting organic traffic.

FAQs
What is keyword research in SEO?
Keyword research is the process of finding and analyzing search terms that users enter into search engines. It helps content creators choose relevant topics and keywords for their website.
Why is keyword research important?
Keyword research helps you understand your audience, identify content opportunities, target relevant searches, and build an effective SEO strategy.
What is a long-tail keyword?
A long-tail keyword is a longer and more specific search phrase. For example, “best SEO tools for small businesses” is more specific than “SEO tools.”
What is search intent?
Search intent is the reason why a person performs a particular search. It can be informational, navigational, commercial, or transactional.
Should I always choose keywords with high search volume?
No. High-volume keywords can have strong competition. A relevant keyword with moderate search volume and lower competition may be a better opportunity, especially for a newer website.
